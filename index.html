Order of Engineers and Architects · Tripoli and North Lebanon نقابة المهندسين في طرابلس والشمال

No engineer in the North can practice without it.

OEA Tripoli is the licensing body for every engineer and architect registered in North Lebanon. It earns from its members and from their work, spends on their pensions, insurance and support, and is governed by people its members elect. This guide maps how all of that connects, using the 2025 RFP for the new system, Law 636/1997 and OEA's own site.

How the money moves

Law 636/1997 · RFP §II, §IV.A

Everything OEA does sits on one of these flows. Money comes in from members and from the projects they sign. It goes back out as pensions, insurance, aid and returns to engineers, and pays for running the institution. Most reports you build will measure one of these arrows.

It's a legal monopoly

Law 636/1997 says no one may practice engineering in Lebanon without being registered in Beirut's or Tripoli's syndicate. Tripoli covers engineers registered in the North Governorate.

Dues buy your vote

Only engineers who paid in full before 1 March can vote or run in that year's elections. Three years unpaid and you are struck off automatically.

Fees follow construction

Because income tracks the number of files, a drop in building permits is a drop in revenue. That is why the permit numbers are what leadership watches first.

Who decides what

Law 636/1997 arts. 16 to 58 · oea-tripoli.org.lb

Members elect everyone above them. The council runs the institution between elections, the delegates hold the purse, and specialised committees take the decisions the law reserves for them. The departments sit under the council and do the daily work.

Everyone
General assemblyالهيئة العامة

All working engineers who paid their dues. Votes every year between late March and mid April for the Naqib, council seats, the disciplinary council and retirement fund seats.

~9,450 eligible voters in 2024
Branchesالفروع

Every engineer belongs to one branch by specialty and practice: consulting civil, consulting architects, consulting electrical, consulting mechanical, public and private employees, and agricultural, contractors and business owners. Each elects a 5-person bureau whose head sits on the council.

Elected bodies
Naqib and councilالنقيب ومجلس النقابة

Naqib plus 12 members, 3-year terms with a third renewed each year. Admits new members, sets rules for registering engineering work, manages the money, supervises every body. Meets at least monthly; the RFP describes the board meeting twice a month.

Naqib Shawki Fatfat · VP Michel Feghali · Secretary Mustafa Fakhreddine · Treasurer Salim Nashabe
Delegates bodyهيئة المندوبين

One delegate per 25 engineers in each branch. Meets in March and September. Approves the accounts and the budget, sets registration and annual fees, appoints the auditor, and must approve any property sale.

Retirement fund committeeلجنة إدارة الصندوق التقاعدي

Naqib plus 8 members, mostly former Naqibs. Runs the pension fund under Law 11/64. Meets every two months to approve retirement and widow requests, and four of its members sign off the budget.

Disciplinary councilالمجلس التأديبي

Naqib or deputy plus 4 elected members. Sanctions run from warning and reprimand to a suspension of up to 3 years, or being struck off.

Working arms
Five departments

Administrative, Technical, Financial, Human resources and Procurement. They receive requests, review documents, take payments and prepare files for whoever has the final say.

Committees and centers

Scientific, financial and administrative committees, the mediation and arbitration center (2022), and the training center.

Federationاتحاد المهندسين اللبنانيين

Joint body with OEA Beirut. Beirut's Naqib presides, Tripoli's is vice president. Coordinates the two orders and handles external relations.

All 56 services

RFP §IV.A and §IV.B

Every service in the RFP, grouped by department. Open one to see who touches it, the rules the system has to enforce, and the reports the RFP asks for.

Rules every technical file shares
  • The engineer must be registered with the right role: consultant for permits and contracts, topographer for survey files, geotechnical engineer for soil reports.
  • Missing documents stop the file; the engineer gets the exact list and resubmits.
  • Overdue payments block the service. Only the head of department can override, and every override is logged with user and time.
  • The system generates a payment slip; finance stamps it; the department validates the file once everything is paid.
